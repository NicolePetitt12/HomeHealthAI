## Details Section

### Why we think this
- Color and texture pattern match
- Location consistent with moisture exposure
- Surface conditions indicate early-stage growth

### What to do now
- Improve airflow in the area
- Clean the surface using appropriate cleaner
- Maintain indoor humidity between 30–50%
- Recheck the area within 72 hours

### Supplies
- Microfiber cloths
- Non-bleach surface cleaner (safe for material)
- Gloves and basic protective gear

### When to call a pro
- If the area spreads beyond 10 sq ft
- If material becomes soft or damaged
- If growth returns after cleaning
